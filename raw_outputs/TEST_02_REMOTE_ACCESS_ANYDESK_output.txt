### 🛡️ Aapke Legal Rights aur Action Plan

Aapke saath jo fraud hua hai (the defrauded amount), usme Indian Law aur RBI ke regulations clearly aapko safeguard karte hain:

#### 1. Kaunse Kanoon Lagu Hote Hain:
- **RBI Circular RBI/2017-18/15 (Zero Liability Rule):** Agar aapne 3 working days me report kiya hai aur credentials voluntarily share nahi kiye, toh **aapki liability ZERO hai**. Paragraph 12 ke mutabik proof ka burden BANK par hai.
- **BNS, 2023 - Section 318:** Online cheating aur financial fraud (IPC 420 replacement).
- **IT Act, 2000 - Section 66D:** Impersonation aur fake identity fraud (3 saal tak ki jail).

#### 2. Turant Lene Wale Steps:
